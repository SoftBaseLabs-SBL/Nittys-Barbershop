03 — The chair

A neighborhood kind of shop.

Nitty's is the kind of shop where the music is good, the conversation is better, and nobody side-eyes you for asking what "two on the sides" actually means. We've been cutting on N. Detroit Ave. for years — fathers, sons, grandfathers, the whole block.

No app to download. No 47-step booking flow. Just push the door open, write your name on the board, and grab a seat. We'll take care of the rest.
